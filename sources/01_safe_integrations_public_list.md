# SAFE public integrations list

Source: https://safe.security/integrations/ (captured Nov 4, 2026)

## What the page says
- Hero: "SAFE integrates with 150+" leading cybersecurity tools.
- Explore section: "over 100+ out-of-the-box integrations to discover and onboard assets and signals."
- Ingestion: telemetry is ingested using "agentless secure passcodes"; the page also mentions "no-code automations." It does not name protocols.
- The page has 22 filter categories (for example SIEM, IAM, EDR), but the cards do not show which category each belongs to.

## Important caveat
The page shows 105 cards, but SAFE claims 150+. Some integrations may load dynamically or may not be listed publicly. A tool missing from this list is NOT proof that SAFE lacks a connector for it. The tool must treat "not on the public list" as "Custom work, confirm with SAFE first."

## Matching notes for the coverage check
- Several tools appear under more than one card (for example "Okta" and "Okta via M365"; four Microsoft Defender variants; six ServiceNow variants). A customer saying "we use Defender" or "we use ServiceNow" should land in "Confirm with SAFE" until the exact product is known.
- Snyk does not appear on this list (Veracode and Qualys SCA do). It is used as the Custom work example.
